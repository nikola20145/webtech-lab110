James Mercer Langston Hughes Famous African American Poet, Langston Hughes Poet, Social activist, Novelist, Playwright, and Columnist Langston Hughes was a playwright, writer, and poet. He developed into a significant voice during the 1920s and 1930s African American literary movement known as the "Harlem Renaissance." His work honors Black people and speaks out against their difficulties.
William Shakespeare Well known author, William Shakespeare Poet, Actor, Author, Man Shakespeare wrote a lot of plays in the Elizabethan and Jacobean periods of British theatre (sometimes called the English Renaissance or the Early Modern Period). Shakespeare authored other things besides plays, though these are undoubtedly his most famous works. Even today, people still like reading Shakespeare's poems.

Leo Tolstoy Russian writer who is regarded as one of the greatest authors of all time. Novelist, Short story writer, Playwright, Essayist Leo Tolstoy, a Russian author best known for his novels War and Peace and Anna Karenina, is regarded as a master of realistic fiction and one of the finest writers in history. He investigated the widest range of perspectives on the human experience while vacillating between skepticism and dogmatism.
